## Strategies

- `tourism_careful`: Interviews every segment in week 1; in week 2 a pilot and, for a segment that books on platforms, a test listing where the interviews point. Picks the segment its evidence favours, builds a product that sells to it and what its interviewees ask for, lists on the platform where that segment books there, sets up its partnerships, builds the website and does outreach where those reach it, prices from interview budget hints (peak at their mean, off season at 85%), rosters to this week's bookings plus a tenth (taking up to two shifts itself), fixes snags, chases invoices, and turns strict only once cancellations are frequent and the calendar busy. It takes a bigger place when guests were turned away in 3 of the last 8 weeks (at the last stage, in peak weeks or above four fifths full, and only with someone on contract where the place needs it) and cash and savings cover the move, six months of the added rent and two months of costs; else it lists the place in the bank's plan. (404.2 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_platform_only`: Plays like tourism_careful but sells only through the platform: no partnerships, website or outreach. (281.9 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_overstaffing`: Plays like tourism_careful but rosters for every product's full capacity, booked or not. (66.5 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_understaffing`: Plays like tourism_careful but rosters for 60% of what is booked. (150.3 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_no_tests`: No interviews or tests: builds every product, the temptation included, and sells to the first segment. (150.8 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_price_blind`: Plays like tourism_careful but keeps the starting price all year. (380.3 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_review_blind`: Plays like tourism_careful but never fixes snags and rosters exactly to bookings, without a margin. (329.6 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_always_flexible`: Plays like tourism_careful with a flexible policy all run. (421.5 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_overworker`: Plays like tourism_careful but plans 65 h every week. (11.0 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_inbox_none`: Plays like tourism_careful but leaves every message to its default. (407.2 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_inbox_effort`: Plays like tourism_careful but answers messages with blind effort: the choice with the most hours it can afford. (360.0 s for 1000 runs)
