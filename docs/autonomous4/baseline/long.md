## Strategies

- `random`: Spends a random 30 to 65 h on random actions in random markets, including tests, pre-sales and invoice chasing, and answers about half of the messages with a random choice. (11.0 s for 1000 runs)
- `build_only`: Builds every feature in list order and fixes bugs. Never talks to anyone. (5.5 s for 1000 runs)
- `sell_first`: Does outreach from week 1 without interviews, by phone in every market, then focuses on the market that asked for most demos. Builds the core feature, then the others in a random order. Chases three late invoices a week. (6.8 s for 1000 runs)
- `validate_then_build`: Interviews every market in week 1. In week 2 pitches one pre-sale batch in the market its interviews favour, runs landing page tests in the top two, and starts the feature everyone asks for. When those tests do not separate the two markets, it runs one larger test in each. Pitches every pre-sale batch its chosen market allows, one a week. Drops a tested market when another drew clearly more landing sign ups (1.5 standard errors), or after two pre-sale batches without a deposit. Builds what its market asks for, sells on its best channel, follows up with up to six open leads there a week, and moves on after 12 weeks of selling without a customer. Sorts the books when the accountant asks, chases three late invoices a week, and reads the inbox (`readInbox`). In long mode it also builds a team. It looks for a hire once its cash and savings grew over the last four weeks, would pay what the hire adds to the monthly loss for 6 months, and at least 26 weeks would be left once the hire starts: a developer while the features its market asks for need 40 h or more, else a salesperson, full time, the best impression. The next search waits until the newest hire has worked 8 weeks. The newest hire is let go once the runway shown falls below 3 months, and a developer once every feature has shipped. It takes a bigger office when its offer arrives and the reason is real: its hiring rule wants someone and every desk or place is taken, or, for the first office, three months that paid all costs; and cash and savings cover the move, six months of the added rent and two months of today's costs. (61.9 s for 1000 runs)
- `validate_then_build_hire`: Plays like validate_then_build, and makes the one hire (a developer while wanted features are unbuilt, else a salesperson) once monthly revenue covers that salary, choosing the best impression. (53.4 s for 1000 runs)
- `validate_then_build_early_hire`: Plays like validate_then_build, and searches for the one hire as soon as its own funnel (leads per hour on the best channel, share of closed leads won, current price) says a developer or salesperson would bring in more than their salary before the last week, assuming no churn and 8 weeks from extra outreach to payment, while cash and savings hold two months of that salary. Takes the role that pays back more, and hires the best impression if it still pays back at that skill. (12.3 s for 1000 runs)
- `presale_max`: Plays like validate_then_build, but pitches every pre-sale batch allowed: three in every market it has not dropped, from week 2, as hours allow. Ranks markets by deposits as well as interviews. (45.8 s for 1000 runs)
- `price_aware`: Plays like validate_then_build and searches for its price from what it sees in its own market. With two or more budget quotes from interviews there, it starts at 0.9 times their average and stays between 0.75 and 1.05 times it. After 2 trials there that said yes or no over the price, it raises the price by a fifth if nobody said no over the price, keeps it if a few did, and lowers it by a step if half did or a customer there left over the price. A price it lowered from is tried again only after two clean windows below it. (56.1 s for 1000 runs)
- `overworker`: Plays like validate_then_build, but plans 65 h every week. (4.7 s for 1000 runs)
- `playtest`: The hand playtest: 5 interviews per market in week 1, builds the three coworking features in weeks 2 to 4, 45 h of in person outreach to coworking in weeks 5 to 7, then every demo plus 15 h of outreach a week. (3.0 s for 1000 runs)
- `validate_then_build_solo`: Plays like validate_then_build and never hires or rents anyone: the bootstrapped solo founder. (52.2 s for 1000 runs)
- `validate_then_build_savings_hire`: Plays like validate_then_build, and makes its first hire on savings: it looks for one as soon as building starts (week 3), while cash and savings hold 3 months of the salary, and keeps that hire until the runway shown falls below 1 month. Later hires follow the reference rule. (22.0 s for 1000 runs)
- `validate_then_build_part_time`: Plays like validate_then_build, with the same team rule, but hires only part time (20 h at half the salary). (55.4 s for 1000 runs)

## Strategies

- `tourism_careful`: Interviews every segment in week 1; in week 2 a pilot and, for a segment that books on platforms, a test listing where the interviews point. Picks the segment its evidence favours, builds a product that sells to it and what its interviewees ask for, lists on the platform where that segment books there, sets up its partnerships, builds the website and does outreach where those reach it, prices from interview budget hints (peak at their mean, off season at 85%), rosters to this week's bookings plus a tenth (taking up to two shifts itself), fixes snags, chases invoices, and turns strict only once cancellations are frequent and the calendar busy. It takes a bigger place when guests were turned away in 3 of the last 8 weeks (at the last stage, in peak weeks or above four fifths full, and only with someone on contract where the place needs it) and cash and savings cover the move, six months of the added rent and two months of costs; else it lists the place in the bank's plan. (60.5 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_platform_only`: Plays like tourism_careful but sells only through the platform: no partnerships, website or outreach. (33.6 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_overstaffing`: Plays like tourism_careful but rosters for every product's full capacity, booked or not. (4.1 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_understaffing`: Plays like tourism_careful but rosters for 60% of what is booked. (45.6 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_no_tests`: No interviews or tests: builds every product, the temptation included, and sells to the first segment. (2.1 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_price_blind`: Plays like tourism_careful but keeps the starting price all year. (49.8 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_review_blind`: Plays like tourism_careful but never fixes snags and rosters exactly to bookings, without a margin. (36.6 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_always_flexible`: Plays like tourism_careful with a flexible policy all run. (44.9 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_overworker`: Plays like tourism_careful but plans 65 h every week. (4.0 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_inbox_none`: Plays like tourism_careful but leaves every message to its default. (32.5 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_inbox_effort`: Plays like tourism_careful but answers messages with blind effort: the choice with the most hours it can afford. (39.4 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_naive_careful`: Plays like tourism_careful without scenario knowledge beyond the screen: messages answered by their declared conditions only, no bridge months or lead times for the loan, and every channel tried for its groups instead of the channel table (tourism fixes, item 17). (38.3 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_contracts_none`: Plays like tourism_careful but books every staff shift by the week and signs no contracts. (42.7 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_permanent`: Plays like tourism_careful but signs only permanent staff contracts where steady work supports them. (43.0 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_seasonal`: Plays like tourism_careful but signs only seasonal staff contracts where the current season supports them. (43.3 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_part_time`: Plays like tourism_careful but signs only part time staff contracts where steady work supports them. (41.6 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_never_move`: Plays like tourism_careful but declines every offer of a bigger place (plan 3, target O1). (43.8 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_always_move`: Plays like tourism_careful but accepts every offer of a bigger place at once, and lists it in the bank plan where there is one (plan 3, target O2). (18.4 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_always_strict`: Plays like tourism_careful with a strict policy all run. (50.9 s for 1000 runs)

## Strategies

- `tourism_careful`: Interviews every segment in week 1; in week 2 a pilot and, for a segment that books on platforms, a test listing where the interviews point. Picks the segment its evidence favours, builds a product that sells to it and what its interviewees ask for, lists on the platform where that segment books there, sets up its partnerships, builds the website and does outreach where those reach it, prices from interview budget hints (peak at their mean, off season at 85%), rosters to this week's bookings plus a tenth (taking up to two shifts itself), fixes snags, chases invoices, and turns strict only once cancellations are frequent and the calendar busy. It takes a bigger place when guests were turned away in 3 of the last 8 weeks (at the last stage, in peak weeks or above four fifths full, and only with someone on contract where the place needs it) and cash and savings cover the move, six months of the added rent and two months of costs; else it lists the place in the bank's plan. (96.2 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_platform_only`: Plays like tourism_careful but sells only through the platform: no partnerships, website or outreach. (45.9 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_overstaffing`: Plays like tourism_careful but rosters for every product's full capacity, booked or not. (27.9 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_understaffing`: Plays like tourism_careful but rosters for 60% of what is booked. (7.8 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_no_tests`: No interviews or tests: builds every product, the temptation included, and sells to the first segment. (103.5 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_price_blind`: Plays like tourism_careful but keeps the starting price all year. (84.6 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_review_blind`: Plays like tourism_careful but never fixes snags and rosters exactly to bookings, without a margin. (51.5 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_always_flexible`: Plays like tourism_careful with a flexible policy all run. (80.1 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_overworker`: Plays like tourism_careful but plans 65 h every week. (3.5 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_inbox_none`: Plays like tourism_careful but leaves every message to its default. (73.4 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_inbox_effort`: Plays like tourism_careful but answers messages with blind effort: the choice with the most hours it can afford. (73.8 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_naive_careful`: Plays like tourism_careful without scenario knowledge beyond the screen: messages answered by their declared conditions only, no bridge months or lead times for the loan, and every channel tried for its groups instead of the channel table (tourism fixes, item 17). (81.1 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_contracts_none`: Plays like tourism_careful but books every staff shift by the week and signs no contracts. (78.5 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_permanent`: Plays like tourism_careful but signs only permanent staff contracts where steady work supports them. (79.4 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_seasonal`: Plays like tourism_careful but signs only seasonal staff contracts where the current season supports them. (79.9 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_part_time`: Plays like tourism_careful but signs only part time staff contracts where steady work supports them. (81.8 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_never_move`: Plays like tourism_careful but declines every offer of a bigger place (plan 3, target O1). (79.6 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_always_move`: Plays like tourism_careful but accepts every offer of a bigger place at once, and lists it in the bank plan where there is one (plan 3, target O2). (14.2 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_always_strict`: Plays like tourism_careful with a strict policy all run. (82.5 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_overborrow`: Plays like tourism_careful but asks the bank for the most it lends, as soon as the plan is written. (38.3 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_reckless`: Plays like tourism_careful but borrows the most the bank lends, with no repayment-free start and the shortest term. (26.2 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_refit_loan`: Plays like tourism_careful and takes a second loan for the own fit-out once guests pay about 20,000 EUR a month. (91.7 s for 1000 runs)

## Strategies

- `tourism_careful`: Interviews every segment in week 1; in week 2 a pilot and, for a segment that books on platforms, a test listing where the interviews point. Picks the segment its evidence favours, builds a product that sells to it and what its interviewees ask for, lists on the platform where that segment books there, sets up its partnerships, builds the website and does outreach where those reach it, prices from interview budget hints (peak at their mean, off season at 85%), rosters to this week's bookings plus a tenth (taking up to two shifts itself), fixes snags, chases invoices, and turns strict only once cancellations are frequent and the calendar busy. It takes a bigger place when guests were turned away in 3 of the last 8 weeks (at the last stage, in peak weeks or above four fifths full, and only with someone on contract where the place needs it) and cash and savings cover the move, six months of the added rent and two months of costs; else it lists the place in the bank's plan. (51.3 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_platform_only`: Plays like tourism_careful but sells only through the platform: no partnerships, website or outreach. (11.6 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_overstaffing`: Plays like tourism_careful but rosters for every product's full capacity, booked or not. (46.4 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_understaffing`: Plays like tourism_careful but rosters for 60% of what is booked. (31.8 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_no_tests`: No interviews or tests: builds every product, the temptation included, and sells to the first segment. (8.9 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_price_blind`: Plays like tourism_careful but keeps the starting price all year. (49.2 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_review_blind`: Plays like tourism_careful but never fixes snags and rosters exactly to bookings, without a margin. (43.3 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_always_flexible`: Plays like tourism_careful with a flexible policy all run. (46.6 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_overworker`: Plays like tourism_careful but plans 65 h every week. (4.7 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_inbox_none`: Plays like tourism_careful but leaves every message to its default. (38.5 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_inbox_effort`: Plays like tourism_careful but answers messages with blind effort: the choice with the most hours it can afford. (49.1 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_naive_careful`: Plays like tourism_careful without scenario knowledge beyond the screen: messages answered by their declared conditions only, no bridge months or lead times for the loan, and every channel tried for its groups instead of the channel table (tourism fixes, item 17). (53.9 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_contracts_none`: Plays like tourism_careful but books every staff shift by the week and signs no contracts. (50.0 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_permanent`: Plays like tourism_careful but signs only permanent staff contracts where steady work supports them. (48.2 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_seasonal`: Plays like tourism_careful but signs only seasonal staff contracts where the current season supports them. (48.6 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_part_time`: Plays like tourism_careful but signs only part time staff contracts where steady work supports them. (48.0 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_never_move`: Plays like tourism_careful but declines every offer of a bigger place (plan 3, target O1). (47.9 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_always_move`: Plays like tourism_careful but accepts every offer of a bigger place at once, and lists it in the bank plan where there is one (plan 3, target O2). (14.2 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_always_strict`: Plays like tourism_careful with a strict policy all run. (48.2 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_overborrow`: Plays like tourism_careful but asks the bank for the most it lends, as soon as the plan is written. (20.7 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_reckless`: Plays like tourism_careful but borrows the most the bank lends, with no repayment-free start and the shortest term. (17.3 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_refit_loan`: Plays like tourism_careful and takes a second loan for the own fit-out once guests pay about 20,000 EUR a month. (35.3 s for 1000 runs)
