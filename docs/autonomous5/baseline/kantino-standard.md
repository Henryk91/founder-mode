## Strategies

- `random`: Spends a random 30 to 65 h on random actions in random markets, including tests, pre-sales and invoice chasing, and answers about half of the messages with a random choice. (15.4 s for 1000 runs)
- `build_only`: Builds every feature in list order and fixes bugs. Never talks to anyone. (3.8 s for 1000 runs)
- `sell_first`: Does outreach from week 1 without interviews, by phone in every market, then focuses on the market that asked for most demos. Builds the core feature, then the others in a random order. Chases three late invoices a week. (6.8 s for 1000 runs)
- `validate_then_build`: Interviews every market in week 1. In week 2 pitches one pre-sale batch in the market its interviews favour, runs landing page tests in the top two, and starts the feature everyone asks for. When those tests do not separate the two markets, it runs one larger test in each. Pitches every pre-sale batch its chosen market allows, one a week. Drops a tested market when another drew clearly more landing sign ups (1.5 standard errors), or after two pre-sale batches without a deposit. Builds what its market asks for, sells on its best channel, follows up with up to six open leads there a week, and moves on after 12 weeks of selling without a customer. Sorts the books when the accountant asks, chases three late invoices a week, and reads the inbox (`readInbox`). (57.4 s for 1000 runs)
- `validate_then_build_hire`: Plays like validate_then_build, and makes the one hire (a developer while wanted features are unbuilt, else a salesperson) once monthly revenue covers that salary, choosing the best impression. (39.4 s for 1000 runs)
- `validate_then_build_early_hire`: Plays like validate_then_build, and searches for the one hire as soon as its own funnel (leads per hour on the best channel, share of closed leads won, current price) says a developer or salesperson would bring in more than their salary before the last week, assuming no churn and 8 weeks from extra outreach to payment, while cash and savings hold two months of that salary. Takes the role that pays back more, and hires the best impression if it still pays back at that skill. (24.1 s for 1000 runs)
- `presale_max`: Plays like validate_then_build, but pitches every pre-sale batch allowed: three in every market it has not dropped, from week 2, as hours allow. Ranks markets by deposits as well as interviews. (26.9 s for 1000 runs)
- `price_aware`: Plays like validate_then_build and searches for its price from what it sees in its own market. With two or more budget quotes from interviews there, it starts at 0.9 times their average and stays between 0.75 and 1.05 times it. After 2 trials there that said yes or no over the price, it raises the price by a fifth if nobody said no over the price, keeps it if a few did, and lowers it by a step if half did or a customer there left over the price. A price it lowered from is tried again only after two clean windows below it. (28.3 s for 1000 runs)
- `overworker`: Plays like validate_then_build, but plans 65 h every week. (4.7 s for 1000 runs)
- `playtest`: The hand playtest: 5 interviews per market in week 1, builds the three coworking features in weeks 2 to 4, 45 h of in person outreach to coworking in weeks 5 to 7, then every demo plus 15 h of outreach a week. (3.0 s for 1000 runs)
