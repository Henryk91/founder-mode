## Strategies

- `leisure_one_room`: Never builds a second room; still maintains the first. (100.2 s for 1000 runs)
- `leisure_quarterly_rooms`: Starts another room every thirteen weeks from opening when it can finance it. (136.7 s for 1000 runs)
- `leisure_theme_blind`: Chooses room themes from a seeded order without matching the market. (143.4 s for 1000 runs)
- `leisure_no_daytime`: Closes weekday daytime sales and keeps the other careful policies. (117.4 s for 1000 runs)
- `leisure_vr_first_year`: Adds the virtual room in the first year with bank help when needed. (119.1 s for 1000 runs)
- `leisure_no_later_loan`: Keeps the careful startup loan policy but funds every later room from retained cash. (108.4 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_careful`: Interviews every segment in week 1; in week 2 a pilot and, for a segment that books on platforms, a test listing where the interviews point. Picks the segment its evidence favours, builds a product that sells to it and what its interviewees ask for, lists on the platform where that segment books there, sets up its partnerships, builds the website and does outreach where those reach it, prices from interview budget hints (peak at their mean, off season at 85%), rosters to this week's bookings plus a tenth (taking up to two shifts itself), fixes snags, chases invoices, and turns strict only once cancellations are frequent and the calendar busy. It takes a bigger place when guests were turned away in 3 of the last 8 weeks (at the last stage, in peak weeks or above four fifths full, and only with someone on contract where the place needs it) and cash and savings cover the move, six months of the added rent and two months of costs; else it lists the place in the bank's plan. (156.0 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_platform_only`: Plays like tourism_careful but sells only through the platform: no partnerships, website or outreach. (111.8 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_overstaffing`: Plays like tourism_careful but rosters for every product's full capacity, booked or not. (69.3 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_understaffing`: Plays like tourism_careful but rosters for 60% of what is booked. (63.3 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_no_tests`: No interviews or tests: builds every product, the temptation included, and sells to the first segment. (138.9 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_price_blind`: Plays like tourism_careful but keeps the starting price all year. (148.8 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_review_blind`: Plays like tourism_careful but never fixes snags and rosters exactly to bookings, without a margin. (146.2 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_always_flexible`: Plays like tourism_careful with a flexible policy all run. (146.0 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_overworker`: Plays like tourism_careful but plans 65 h every week. (4.1 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_inbox_none`: Plays like tourism_careful but leaves every message to its default. (130.5 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_inbox_effort`: Plays like tourism_careful but answers messages with blind effort: the choice with the most hours it can afford. (145.3 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_naive_careful`: Plays like tourism_careful without scenario knowledge beyond the screen: messages answered by their declared conditions only, no bridge months or lead times for the loan, and every channel tried for its groups instead of the channel table (tourism fixes, item 17). (140.8 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_contracts_none`: Plays like tourism_careful but books every staff shift by the week and signs no contracts. (145.7 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_permanent`: Plays like tourism_careful but signs only permanent staff contracts where steady work supports them. (145.7 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_seasonal`: Plays like tourism_careful but signs only seasonal staff contracts where the current season supports them. (145.1 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_part_time`: Plays like tourism_careful but signs only part time staff contracts where steady work supports them. (147.0 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_never_move`: Plays like tourism_careful but declines every offer of a bigger place (plan 3, target O1). (147.1 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_always_move`: Plays like tourism_careful but accepts every offer of a bigger place at once, and lists it in the bank plan where there is one (plan 3, target O2). (145.0 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_always_strict`: Plays like tourism_careful with a strict policy all run. (135.7 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_overborrow`: Plays like tourism_careful but asks the bank for the most it lends, as soon as the plan is written. (130.6 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_reckless`: Plays like tourism_careful but borrows the most the bank lends, with no repayment-free start and the shortest term. (127.3 s for 1000 runs)
- `tourism_refit_loan`: Plays like tourism_careful and takes a second loan for the own fit-out once guests pay about 20,000 EUR a month. (144.9 s for 1000 runs)
